📖 Biography

🙋‍♂️ I am currently a visiting PhD student at Singapore Management University under the supervision of Prof. Guansong Pang, and belong to the Machine Learning & Applications (MaLA) Lab at SMU. I am pursuing my PhD degree at University of Wollongong, advised by Prof. Jun Shen and Prof. Jun Yan. Previously, I received my Master's degree at Xidian University in 2023.

My research interests lie in using Deep Learning to build Trustworthy AI Systems. Currently, I focus on Efficient/Faithful Agentic Reasoning (Attribution & Intervention) and Generalized Anomaly/Risk Detection. Besides, I also explore following areas:

  • 🎨 Visual Representation Learning
  • 🚨 Out-of-Distribution Generalization
  • 🍟 Multimodal Large Language Model
  • 🤖 Lifelong Learning of Large Language Model

😄 I'm excited to work with colleagues in the Machine Learning & Applications (MaLA) Lab at Singapore Management University, focusing on Agentic Reasoning and Anomaly Detection for facilitating Trustworthy AI.
